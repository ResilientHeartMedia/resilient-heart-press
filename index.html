WHY RESILIENT HEART PRESS

A publishing house built around creative ownership.

Every story begins with an author and a vision. We believe creative ownership means protecting that vision and giving authors the freedom to shape how their work is presented to the world.

Modern tools can expand what creators are able to do, but they will never replace the imagination, empathy, and lived experience that make stories matter. We embrace innovation, while keeping human authorship at the heart of everything we do.

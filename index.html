Love Lies

After her high-society fiancé leaves her homeless and drowning in debt, Amy Beckett is fighting to save her last sanctuary: her beloved café. Her opponent? Matthew Warren, a merciless senior counsel hired by a ruthless billionaire to guarantee Amy's ruin. But Matthew’s courtroom armor cracks when he recognizes his own hidden scars in Amy’s desperate fight. What begins as a mission to destroy her becomes a forbidden alliance and an all-consuming romance. Ultimately, Amy must decide if she can trust the man hired to break her, and Matthew must prove that, this time, love isn't just another lie.

Love Lies is a high-stakes contemporary romance about surviving betrayal and finding fire in the most unexpected places.

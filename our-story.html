We don't fear the future of publishing. We are writing it.

At Resilient Heart Press, we aren't just publishing books; we are dismantling the outdated barriers to human creativity. In a rapidly shifting industry, we stand proudly at the intersection of timeless storytelling and modern innovation.

Rather than resisting the advancements that shape the future of literature, we harness them. We believe that modern technology is not a threat to artistry, but a powerful engine for it. We refuse to operate from a place of fear. Instead, we champion innovation as the ultimate catalyst to streamline the independent publishing process and elevate an author's most ambitious ideas. We empower our writers to unleash their creativity, refine their craft, and share their truest voices with the world.

We are a forward-thinking publishing house dedicated to championing the storyteller and delivering exceptional books to passionate readers. We believe that when you give creators access to the future, you don't replace the soul of a story, you set it free.

Joelle founded Resilient Heart Press and Resilient Heart Records out of an unshakeable devotion to creative freedom.

Joelle Habbaki is a Lebanese-Canadian author, songwriter, music producer, and the driving force behind Resilient Heart Media. Watching the growing industry hostility toward artists who embrace cutting-edge technology, she established this overarching home for Resilient Heart Press and Resilient Heart Records. Her goal is to create a protected ecosystem where storytellers and musicians can experiment without fear of backlash.

Fluent in English, French, and Arabic, Joelle fuels this mission with her own relentless creative drive. She is unapologetically a one-woman show and a master of her own process. From writing her award-recognized contemporary romance novels, Surrender and Love Lies, to designing her book and music cover art, producing video trailers, and releasing bilingual music that streams across more than 25 global platforms, she knows exactly what it takes to execute an ambitious vision from the ground up. As a fully independent creator who orchestrates every detail of her own releases, she intimately understands the value of modern technology.

“This is a haven where creators are championed, not judged, for utilizing the tools of the future. And my mission is to give artists the ultimate freedom to experiment, push boundaries, and let their imaginations soar higher than was ever possible before.”
— Joelle Habbaki

Resilient Heart Symbol

Our logo is inspired by Kintsugi, the art of mending broken pieces with gold. It was born from a simple fact: Joelle writes romance for the resilient heart. Today, that core philosophy has expanded into a broader stance for creative freedom, serving as the unshakeable foundation for both the publishing house and the record label. The gold-mended heart is a visual promise to the modern creator. It is a reminder that navigating restrictive mindsets doesn't break your artistic spirit; it mends it with gold, making your work stronger, bolder, and completely unshakeable.

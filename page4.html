SHATTERED

Small-Town Romance Novel

Shattered is the first book in the St. Austell Duet.
Emma and Edward's story concludes in Surrender.

"Love the way you've written the characters. They feel very real. Well-written too!" — JENNIFER R., PAGE TURNER AWARDS JUDGE

"I cannot put this book down! Loving the twists and turns, this is definitely a page-turner!" — JMCARRON

In the quiet town of St. Austell, Emma Kinsley is just trying to keep her head above water. Suffocating under the weight of an unexpected tragedy, she retreats to the moonlit shores of Carlyon Bay just to breathe. She is expecting solitude.

Instead, a stranger steps out of the dark.

Edward Donald traded the towering skyscrapers of New York for the rugged Cornish coast, burying his truth behind the quiet facade of a local hotel worker. Guarded and intensely private, he is a dangerous distraction from the fiercely independent future she is fighting to build.

And the absolute last thing Emma needs.

But Edward sees the broken pieces she tries so hard to hide. What begins as a hesitant, shared solace in the dark deepens as Edward recognizes a woman worth destroying his carefully built peace for. Despite her resistance, his quiet obsession chips away at her walls, making him a fiercely protective anchor in her chaotic world.

Yet, Edward's refuge is built on a foundation of secrets.

When his Manhattan life is violently forced back to the surface, Emma is pushed to the breaking point.

Can she trust the man who became her anchor?
Or will his hidden truths leave her shattered?

About Joelle Habbaki

An award-finalist author of high-stakes contemporary romance, Joelle Habbaki writes for the resilient heart. She is drawn to the fracture points in life. To those devastating moments where everything breaks. And she believes the hardest chapters often lead to the most beautiful beginnings.

Best known for her Page Turner Award-nominated St. Austell Duet (Shattered and Surrender) and her acclaimed standalone Love Lies, Joelle writes for readers who crave real characters and protective heroes. She builds deeply immersive, emotional worlds where the journey to a "happily ever after" is hard-won, wholly deserved, and love is the only thing that can put the pieces back together.

Joelle’s storytelling extends far beyond the written word. When she isn't writing her next emotional wrecking ball of a romance, this independent creator is designing her own cover art, directing cinematic book trailers, and producing bilingual music that streams across more than 25 global platforms. She doesn't just write stories. She builds complete, immersive experiences, pioneering a new era of limitless creative freedom.

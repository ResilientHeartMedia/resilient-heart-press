SUBMISSIONS

Your story matters. Tell it your way.

Resilient Heart Press was founded by a multimedia creator who wanted the freedom to make ambitious work without having to compromise the way she created it. As we grow, we hope to extend that same freedom to other remarkable storytellers.

A Curated List, Not a Catalog

Resilient Heart Press is intentionally boutique. We are not trying to build the largest possible list. We want to build a thoughtful one, with books we genuinely believe in and authors we can work with personally.

When submissions reopen, unsolicited manuscripts will be welcome. You do not need an agent to submit to us. We believe a remarkable story should be able to find its way to a publisher, even if its author has not yet found representation.

Our publishing list is carefully curated, and we are deliberate about the number of works we take on. Submission windows may therefore open periodically and may be limited in capacity, allowing us to approach each manuscript with curiosity, care, and the personal attention it deserves.

What We Look For

When submissions reopen, we will be looking for stories that stay with us. Stories with heart, originality, emotional depth, and a strong sense of identity.

Move us.

We want characters who feel human, complicated, and capable of change. We want stories that leave an emotional imprint.

Have something of their own to say.

We are not looking for another version of what is already selling. We want distinctive voices and perspectives that feel personal to the author behind them.

Take creative risks.

That does not necessarily mean being experimental for the sake of being experimental. It means being willing to follow the story where it needs to go.

Believe in transformation.

The Resilient Heart name began with stories about characters who are tested by life and changed by what they survive. That spirit remains at the heart of what we do.

Have been thoughtfully crafted.

We value writers who care about their words, their characters, their structure, and the experience they are creating for the reader.

What We Don't Believe
  • We do not believe every good story needs to fit the same mould.
  • We do not believe an author's tools determine the value of their work.
  • And we do not believe originality requires abandoning everything familiar.
A remarkable story can be commercial or unconventional, genre-driven or difficult to categorize. What matters most to us is that it feels intentional, honest, and alive.
What About Genre?

Romance is where Resilient Heart Press began, and it remains an important part of our identity. But our long-term vision is not limited to one genre.

As the press grows, we may consider stories beyond romance when they feel genuinely at home within the Resilient Heart philosophy.

We are interested in stories about people who are changed by what they experience, stories that explore resilience, connection, identity, love, loss, ambition, or the many ways people become who they are.

Genre matters. Heart matters more.
Our Submission Philosophy

We want the submission process to feel human.

We know how much courage it takes to place your work in someone else's hands. We will always aim to approach submissions with curiosity, respect, and an open mind.

We also understand what it means to create independently. We are interested in the person behind the manuscript as much as the manuscript itself.

We are not looking for authors who fit Resilient Heart. We are looking for stories that belong here.

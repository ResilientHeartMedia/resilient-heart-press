SURRENDER

Billionaire Romance Novel

Surrender is the final installment in the St. Austell Duet.
Emma and Edward's journey begins in Shattered.

"Tight and tense. Good writing." — ROBIN K., PAGE TURNER AWARDS JUDGE

"You feel like you are stepping in between the images and observing the story as it unfolds." — DAWN C.

Emma Kinsley is pouring the shattered pieces of her heart into her new interior design career. She survived the brutal shock of Edward Hughes's true identity and the agonizing tabloid rumors of his Manhattan fiancée. She has mastered the art of pretending she is whole, swearing she will never let the man who broke her get close enough to do it again.

Until her phone lights up in the dead of night.

An ocean away, Edward's carefully controlled world has completely collapsed. The vicious public exposure of his adoption was only the beginning. Faced with the tragic death of his biological mother, the Manhattan billionaire is stripped of everything he thought he knew.

Hiding the truth cost him the only woman he ever loved.

Broken and desperate for the one person who truly sees him, he calls Emma with an unthinkable plea: cross the Atlantic and stand by his side as he faces the ghosts of his past. To do it, she'll have to walk straight back into the high-society snake pit that tore them apart in the first place.

But Edward has nothing left to hide, and everything to lose.

He is playing for keeps. And Emma is trapped between two impossible choices:

Keep pretending she can actually survive without him?
Or surrender to a second chance that could ruin her for good?

About Joelle Habbaki

An award-finalist author of high-stakes contemporary romance, Joelle Habbaki writes for the resilient heart. She is drawn to the fracture points in life. To those devastating moments where everything breaks. And she believes the hardest chapters often lead to the most beautiful beginnings.

Best known for her Page Turner Award-nominated St. Austell Duet (Shattered and Surrender) and her acclaimed standalone Love Lies, Joelle writes for readers who crave real characters and protective heroes. She builds deeply immersive, emotional worlds where the journey to a "happily ever after" is hard-won, wholly deserved, and love is the only thing that can put the pieces back together.

Joelle’s storytelling extends far beyond the written word. When she isn't writing her next emotional wrecking ball of a romance, this independent creator is designing her own cover art, directing cinematic book trailers, and producing bilingual music that streams across more than 25 global platforms. She doesn't just write stories. She builds complete, immersive experiences, pioneering a new era of limitless creative freedom.
